How NeuroScan AI Works

A rigorous, reproducible computational pipeline from raw scan ingestion to transparent explainability.

01

Intake & Preprocessing

Standardizes MRI slice to 128×128 pixels in 3-channel RGB format, normalized to [0.0, 1.0] pixel intensities without altering morphological structures.

02

Deep Convolutional Inference

The pretrained VGG16 architecture extracts multi-scale hierarchical feature maps, fed into dense classification layers with Softmax activation across 4 diagnostic classes.

03

Grad-CAM Explainability

Computes class-discriminative gradients w.r.t. layer block5_conv3 to produce a spatial activation heatmap highlighting anatomical regions influencing the decision.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Neural Network Architecture & Methodology

Designed with clinical transparency, transfer learning, and gradient-based interpretability.

BACKBONE

VGG16 Transfer Learning

Leverages deep 16-layer convolutional filters pretrained on ImageNet to extract robust low-level edge features and high-level morphological representations from medical scans.

REGULARIZATION

Dense Head & Dropout

Features are flattened and passed through a 512-neuron fully connected layer with ReLU activation, accompanied by 50% Dropout regularization to mitigate overfitting.

INTERPRETABILITY

Genuine Grad-CAM

Real-time calculation of gradients from the final convolutional layer (block5_conv3) mapped directly onto the original image space using Jet colormap synthesis.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Stateless In-Memory I/O

MRI byte streams are processed entirely in volatile memory buffers (io.BytesIO). Scans and patient identities are never persisted to disk, preserving confidentiality.

ACADEMIC RESEARCH INITIATIVE

About NeuroScan AI

NeuroScan AI was engineered to explore the intersection of medical neuroimaging and explainable deep learning (XAI). The project demonstrates how high-capacity convolutional neural networks can be paired with gradient-based interpretability algorithms to assist biomedical researchers and computer vision practitioners in understanding model attention.

Academic Prototype Disclaimer:

This software is developed strictly for research, educational, and demonstration purposes. It has not undergone clinical trials, FDA review, or regulatory clearance as a medical device. It should never be used as a primary diagnostic tool or a substitute for expert medical consultation.
